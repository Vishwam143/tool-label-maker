# Tool Label Maker

Read a data sheet photo, then put each tool’s serial number, part number and name on your tool photo.

## 1. Data sheet photo

Upload the photo of your list. If it is upside down or sideways, press Rotate until the text is upright.

Click or drop the data sheet photo

Check the rows after reading. You can fix any serial number, name or part number by typing in it.

## 2. Tool photo

Upload the photo of the tools, then type the serial numbers in the order they sit from left to right.

Click or drop the tool photo

Serial numbers, left to right

A range like 131-136 works, or a list separated by commas. The labels appear as soon as both the photo and serial numbers are ready.
